# 🛵 Swiggy Power BI Dashboard 
Hi, I'm Md. Yeakub – and this is one of my favorite data storytelling projects, built around Swiggy's food delivery data. I created this interactive dashboard using Power BI to Overview, User Performance, City Overview and Resturant Analysis across India.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This case study simulates an Associate Product Manager (APM) scenario where I led the product planning and sprint execution for a Swiggy Order Analysis Dashboard.

**Key Responsibilities:**
- Managed the product backlog 
- Built dynamic dashboards with **Power BI**
- Used **Microsoft Project** for timeline and release planning

---

## 🎯 Objective

The main goal was to extract **actionable business insights** from Swiggy’s historical food delivery data, focusing on:

- Sales performance across cities and product categories
- Customer acquisition, engagement, churn patterns
- Regional and demographic consumption behavior
- Business strategy recommendations based on data

## 📊 Dashboards Created

### 🔹 I. Home Page – Company Overview
- **Total Sales:** ₹987M
- **Orders:** 150K+
- **Quantity Sold:** 2M+ items
- **Revenue by Category:** Veg > Non-Veg > Others

💡 *Insight:* Veg dishes dominate in terms of revenue. Indicates a strong vegetarian customer base or higher value per Veg order.

---

### 🔹 II. User Performance Dashboard
- **Total Users:** 100K
- **Active Users:** 78K
- **Net Loss:** 21K users
- **Top Age Group:** 23–25 years

💡 *Insight:* High churn rate among users. The 20–30 age group is most engaged—ideal for retention campaigns and re-targeting.

---

### 🔹 III. City Performance Dashboard
- **Top Cities by Sales:** Tirupati, Electronic City, Baner
- **High Engagement Cities:** Bikaner, Noida-1, Indirapuram

💡 *Insight:* Cities with high ratings and engagement show strong potential for hyperlocal promotions and service expansion.

---

### 🔹 III. Restaurant Analysis Dashboard
- **Top Restaurant are available in Bikaner.
- **Top Cuisine by Sales: ** North Indian, Chinese.

💡 *Insight:* Bikaner leads with the highest number of restaurants, followed by Noida-1, with North Indian and Chinese cuisines dominating both in availability and top sales, while Domino’s Pizza holds the highest customer rating.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- **Churn Management:** Launch retention campaigns, loyalty programs, personalized offers
- **Bolt Delivery Focus:** Educate users, target metro hubs with fast-paced lifestyles
- **City Strategy:** Double down on high-performing cities; improve service in underperformers
- **Product Strategy:** Bundle lesser-performing “Others” category with meals
- **Youth Engagement:** Use gamification, meme marketing, and referral rewards
